Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our programs, when you participate in activities on the website, or otherwise when you contact us.

  • Personal Identification Data: Name, email address, phone number, and location.
  • Inquiry Details: Information provided when filling out volunteer forms, donation inquiries, or partnership requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for purposes based on legitimate social and organizational interests:

  • To respond to user inquiries and offer support to volunteers and partners.
  • To manage administrative operations and communicate impact updates.
  • To enforce our terms, conditions, and policies for security and legal compliance.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Noventra Foundation follows standard log file procedures. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes IP addresses, browser type, ISP, date/time stamp, and referring pages. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

4. Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. All personal details provided to Noventra Foundation remain strictly confidential and protected.
