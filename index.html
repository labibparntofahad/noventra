Who We Are

A foundation built on opportunity

Noventra Foundation is a social development organization committed to creating sustainable opportunities for individuals, families, and communities. We believe that meaningful development begins by empowering people with access to opportunity, knowledge, resources, and dignity.

We are beginning our work in Bangladesh, with a long-term aim of expanding our development programs regionally and internationally as our foundation grows.

Empowerment

Helping people become more economically and socially independent.

Opportunity

Creating pathways toward education, entrepreneurship, and sustainable livelihoods.

Sustainability

Building long-term solutions rather than temporary assistance.

Vision

A world where every person has the opportunity, resources, and confidence to build a better future.

Mission

To empower underserved communities through responsible financial inclusion, education, skills, entrepreneurship, and sustainable social development.

Financial Inclusion

From small opportunity to sustainable growth

Our financial inclusion vision begins with responsible small-scale financing that can help individuals start or strengthen income-generating activities. As beneficiaries grow and demonstrate sustainable progress, future programs may expand toward larger financing opportunities.
